## 2024-05-27 - SmartDownloadPrompt Accessibility
**Learning:** The SmartDownloadPrompt component has an icon-only dismiss button and a custom progress bar without ARIA attributes. Memory explicitly states: 'Ensure progress bars (such as in `SmartDownloadPrompt`) implement `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, and `aria-valuemax` attributes for screen reader accessibility.' and 'For accessibility, ensure modal components like `SmartDownloadPrompt` use `role="region"` for their container, `aria-pressed` for selection buttons (preserving standard Tab navigation over `role="radio"`), and explicit `aria-label`s for icon-only buttons.'
**Action:** Add `aria-label` to the dismiss button, and `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, and `aria-valuemax` to the progress bar container, `role="region"` to the modal container, and `aria-pressed` to the model selection buttons.
