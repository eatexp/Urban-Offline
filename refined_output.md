Hypothermia - Wikipedia Jump to content Hypothermia From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia Medical emergency where the body loses heat faster than it can produce it Hypothermia Specialty Emergency medicine Hypothermia is a medical emergency that occurs when your body loses heat faster than it can produce it, causing a dangerously low body temperature.
